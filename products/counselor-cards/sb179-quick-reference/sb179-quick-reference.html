THE RULE  +  WHAT COUNTS AS DIRECT (80%)

SB 179 (2021) · TEC §33.006

⚖️ THE RULE EXPLAINED

Senate Bill 179 (87th Texas Legislature, 2021) requires Texas school counselors to spend at least 80% of total work time on duties that are components of the district's comprehensive school counseling program (TEC §33.005). No more than 20% may go to non-program and administrative duties.

A district that assigns a counselor below 80% must adopt a board policy explaining why, listing the non-program duties, and stating the percentage of time set for program duties.

🎯 WEEKLY TIME TARGETS (40-HOUR WEEK)

• Direct services: 32 hours minimum (80%)
• Indirect services: 8 hours maximum (20%)
• Per day: ~6.4 hours direct, ~1.6 hours indirect
• Track in 15-minute increments for accuracy

WHAT'S INDIRECT (20% MAX)  +  WHAT DOESN'T COUNT

Know the difference — it's the whole audit

🟡 INDIRECT — 20% MAXIMUM

  • Program management & coordination
  • Committee meetings (504, MTSS, RTI)
  • Data entry & reporting
  • Professional development attendance
  • Consultation with admin about the program
  • Curriculum development
  • Testing coordination (STAAR, state assessments)
  • ⚠️ Scheduling duties — should be minimized per TEC §33.006
  • ⚠️ Hallway/cafeteria duty — NOT counseling — should be 0%
  • ⚠️ Clerical tasks assigned by admin — NOT counseling

❌ DOES NOT COUNT AT ALL

  • ✖ Substitute teaching
  • ✖ Bus duty
  • ✖ Lunch duty
  • ✖ Standardized test administration
  • ✖ Disciplinary proceedings (unless counseling-related)
  • ✖ Clerical/administrative tasks unrelated to the counseling program
⚠️ "If it could be done by someone without a counseling degree, it's not counseling."
