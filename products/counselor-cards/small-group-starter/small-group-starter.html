👫 FRIENDSHIP SKILLS

Grades K-2Group 4-66-8 sessionsASCA B-SS 1, 2
OPENING ACTIVITIES
  • Name game with compliments
  • "What makes a good friend?" brainstorm
  • Friendship recipe (ingredients of friendship)
KEY SKILLS
Sharing, turn-taking, using kind words, including others

😤 ANGER MANAGEMENT

Grades K-5Group 4-68 sessionsASCA B-SS 2, 9
OPENING ACTIVITIES
  • Volcano drawing (when do you erupt?)
  • Feelings thermometer check-in
  • Anger iceberg (what's underneath?)
KEY SKILLS
Identifying triggers, deep breathing, walking away, using I-statements

😰 WORRY WARRIORS (ANXIETY)

Grades 2-5Group 4-66-8 sessionsASCA B-SS 2, 7
OPENING ACTIVITIES
  • Worry jar (write worries, put them away)
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
  • "Is this a BIG worry or a little worry?"
KEY SKILLS
Grounding techniques, positive self-talk, separating real vs. imagined fears

💔 CHANGING FAMILIES (DIVORCE)

Grades K-5Group 4-66 sessionsASCA B-SS 2, 7, 8
OPENING ACTIVITIES
  • Two-homes drawing
  • "Things that stay the same" list
  • Feelings check-in with emojis
KEY SKILLS
Expressing feelings, understanding it's not their fault, coping with change
